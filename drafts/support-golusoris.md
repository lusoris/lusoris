## Support

**Why it matters.** golusoris is a composable Go framework: opt-in fx modules for what a production backend needs (config, log, db, http, otel, jobs, cache, auth, k8s), spread over 31 Go modules that `make verify-all` builds, lints, scans and race-tests on every change.

**What support funds.**
- CI time for the required Linux, macOS and Windows matrix.
- Keeping all 31 Go modules green under `make verify-all` on every change.
