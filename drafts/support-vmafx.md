## 💖 Support VMAFx

**Why it matters.** VMAFx is a fork of Netflix/vmaf that keeps Netflix's reference scores and checks every GPU backend (CUDA, SYCL, HIP, Metal) and SIMD path against the CPU scores, so a score means the same on every device.

**What support funds.**
- CI time, the largest running cost: build matrix, numerical-exactness gates, sanitizer jobs.
- Cloud GPU test time on hardware the project does not own.
- Next, an AI workstation for training and evaluating the tiny models.

Sponsor on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/lusoris) (USD), any amount; no support or priority is sold. Also on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/lusoris) or [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/Lusoris) (EUR). Open Collective: pending.
Sponsors are thanked in [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md); [Support VMAFx](docs/support-vmafx.md) explains what it pays for.
