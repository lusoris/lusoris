# Tekmora

**Evidence before compute.**

Open tools for building software with AI agents,
where a claim counts only with evidence behind it.

**The name.** From Greek *tekmor* (Homeric *tekmor*): a set goal, and the sign that shows the way to it.
Said TEK-mo-ra.

## Projects

- **Diallak**: a community bot that answers from the
  documentation and cites it.
  https://github.com/tekmora/diallak
- More projects are moving here from their earlier
  home. Each arrives with its first release under its
  new name.

The libraries these projects build on live at
[Plurano](https://github.com/plurano).
